
# My Favorite Korean Drama Series

## Under the Queen's Umbrella (슈룹)

**Under the Queen's Umbrella** is one of my favorite Korean drama series. It is a historical drama that tells the story of Queen Im Hwa-ryeong, a strong and determined queen who does everything she can to protect her children and prepare them for their responsibilities in the royal family.

### About the Series

The story takes place during the Joseon Dynasty, where the royal family faces challenges, competition, secrets, and political conflicts. Queen Hwa-ryeong struggles to raise her sons properly while dealing with the Queen Dowager and other people who want power and influence in the palace.

> A mother's love, courage, and determination can overcome even the greatest challenges.

### Series Information

- **Title:** Under the Queen's Umbrella
- **Korean Title:** 슈룹 (Shuroop)
- **Release Year:** 2022
- **Country:** South Korea
- **Genre:** Historical, Drama, Comedy
- **Director:** Kim Hyung-sik
- **Writer:** Park Ba-ra
- **Network:** tvN
- **Total Episodes:** 16

### Main Cast and Characters

1. **Kim Hye-soo** – Queen Im Hwa-ryeong
2. **Kim Hae-sook** – Queen Dowager
3. **Choi Won-young** – King Lee Ho
4. **Moon Sang-min** – Grand Prince Seongnam
5. **Bae In-hyuk** – Crown Prince
6. **Yoon Sang-hyun** – Grand Prince Muan
7. **Yoo Seon-ho** – Grand Prince Gyeseong

### Why I Love This K-Drama

I love this Korean drama because it shows the importance of family, love, sacrifice, and courage. Queen Hwa-ryeong is an inspiring character because she is willing to set aside her pride and royal dignity just to protect her children.

I also enjoy the historical setting, beautiful costumes, emotional scenes, unexpected twists, and the struggles between the characters. The series teaches us that being a good parent is not about being perfect but about supporting, understanding, and guiding your children.

### My Favorite Things About the Series

- Queen Hwa-ryeong's intelligence and bravery.
- The strong relationship between a mother and her children.
- The friendship and loyalty among the princes.
- The beautiful royal costumes and palace setting.
- The unexpected plot twists and exciting conflicts.
- The meaningful lessons about family and responsibility.

### Lessons I Learned

1. Family should always be valued and protected.
2. A person's abilities do not determine their worth.
3. Courage is important when facing challenges.
4. Everyone deserves understanding and support.
5. True leadership requires kindness, wisdom, and responsibility.

---

### My Overall Review

I highly recommend **Under the Queen's Umbrella** to anyone who enjoys Korean historical dramas. It is not just a story about royalty and competition for the throne; it is also a meaningful story about motherhood, family, love, and sacrifice.

For me, this series is special because it reminds us that a mother's love can serve as an umbrella that protects her children from life's storms.

**Rating:** 10/10 ⭐
